## What is ShutterCoach?

ShutterCoach gives you honest, encouraging feedback on your photos — like having a photography mentor in your pocket (and your browser). Available as a native **iOS app** and a **Chrome extension**, both powered by the same AI mentor, Luna.

---

<!-- 5. iOS App Features -->
## iOS App

- **AI-Powered Critique** — Detailed analysis across 6 skills: composition, lighting, exposure, focus, color, and storytelling
- **Photo DNA** — A personalized photography profile that evolves as your skills grow
- **Daily Challenges** — Fresh prompts every day with streak tracking to build better habits
- **Chat with Luna** — Ask follow-up questions and get deeper guidance from your AI mentor
- **Batch Import & Cull** — Import multiple photos, cull your library with AI assistance
- **Shoot Reports** — Session-level insights across a batch of photos
- **Critique Revision Engine** — Compare before/after shots to see how you've improved
- **Profile Tab** — Your photography hub with avatars, stats, and progress visualization
- **Achievements & Milestones** — 5 achievement categories to celebrate your progress
- **Aurora UI** — Streaming feedback with Aurora morphing animations and Liquid Glass design
- **Guided Onboarding** — 5-day guided journey with daily notifications and tips
- **Offline History** — Access your last 500 critiques without internet
- **Privacy-First** — Your photos are never stored on our servers

<!-- 6. Chrome Extension Features -->
## Chrome Extension

- **Right-Click Any Photo** — Get instant AI feedback on any image you find on the web
- **Two Coaching Modes** — **Study** (learn from any photo online) and **Coach** (upload your own work)
- **Follow-Up Chat** — Continue the conversation with Luna after every critique
- **Share Cards** — Canvas-rendered share cards to show off your scores
- **Critique History** — Browse past analyses stored locally
- **Aurora Glass UI** — Same premium glass card design and aurora background as the iOS app
- **Upgrade Gate** — 5-day trial, then one-time $9.99 purchase via Stripe

<!-- 7. How it Works (3-step) -->
## How It Works

| Step | | |
| :--: | :-- | :-- |
| **1** | **Share Your Shot** | Pick a photo from your library (iOS) or right-click any image on the web (Chrome) |
| **2** | **Get Honest Feedback** | AI analyzes composition, lighting, exposure, focus, color, and storytelling |
| **3** | **Level Up** | Chat with Luna, track your Photo DNA, and build a photography habit |
