ShutterCoach reviews your photos and tells you what's working, what isn't, and one specific thing to try on your next shot. The coach is Luna, an AI. She reads a photo as a whole first, then skill by skill: composition, lighting, exposure, focus, color and storytelling.

This repository holds the public issue tracker and discussions. The app itself is closed source. The current facts, including prices, are always on [shuttercoach.app](https://shuttercoach.app).

## What it does

**iPhone and iPad app**

- **Critiques** that stream in as Luna reads, with her notes drawn on the photo
- **Follow-up questions** about any critique
- **Re-shoot and compare**: take the shot again and see what changed
- **Shoot review**: go through a batch with Keep and Pass, then get a report on the whole shoot
- **A camera** with a live histogram, focus peaking, zebras and Luna's cues
- **Planner** for light, places and shoots, plus 12 photography calculators and pose references
- **Photo DNA, practice and challenges** that track how your photos develop

**Chrome extension**

- **Study**: right-click any photo on the web and choose "Ask Luna about this photo" to learn why it works
- **Coach**: upload your own photo for feedback
- **Follow-up questions and saved history** in Chrome's side panel

## Pricing (US)

- Your first successful critique starts a **7-day Pro evaluation**. No payment details, no automatic billing.
- After that, **3 free single-photo critiques every 30 days**, each with one follow-up question. Unused critiques don't roll over, and failed requests don't count.
- **Pro** is $9.99 a month, $69.99 a year or $149.99 once.
- If you bought lifetime or Early Bird access before 2.0, you keep Pro.

Prices in other countries are set by the App Store. See [shuttercoach.app/pricing](https://shuttercoach.app/pricing/) for the current offer.

## Privacy

Photos are sent for analysis only when you ask, and they aren't stored on our servers. Location data is removed before upload. See the [privacy policy](https://shuttercoach.app/privacy/).
